Action research · 2024 – ongoing

Teaching After the Feed

Students meet most images inside algorithmically ranked surfaces. The lesson asks for something different: sustained looking, accumulation, return. This is not a problem of attention spans — it is a problem of structural legibility.

This project builds classroom tools for both sides of that gap, tests them in one A-Level course, and shares every one of them freely.

The two models Straight to the tools
Model A

Teaching with the feed

The lesson is shaped like a curated discovery. Sequences that borrow the feed’s structural logic — adjacency, contrast, incremental reveal — without handing control to a platform. Students meet examples before they receive explanations.

Constellation Board live

Place images in relation. Find what connects them.

The Travelling Image live

Watch an image lose data every time it is shared.

Model B

Teaching against the feed

The lesson is shaped like a library, not a queue. Archive-first structures that resist novelty in favour of return, reuse, and accumulation. Students begin from a stable reference bank and revisit before anything new is introduced.

Close Reading live

Structured visual analysis before interpretation.

Curatewith.art live

A class-built, revisitable image bank.

Reflow testing

Course planning that knows your calendar.

Attention Heatmap and Cadence LMS serve both models; Lockdown Browser and the Invigilation Clock are classroom infrastructure.
