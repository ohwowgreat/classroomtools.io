Constellation Board

Place images in relation. Find what connects them.

A Warburg-inspired tool for arranging images spatially and discovering the patterns that emerge from adjacency. Students juxtapose works, articulate connections, and build arguments before they are given explanations. The board becomes a visible record of their thinking.

Open Constellation Board ↗ Source on GitHub

constellation.classroomtools.io · no accounts · free

In the classroom

Three moves, one lesson.

1

Seed the board

Drop in the images for today — works, details, studies. No accounts, no setup; the board lives in the browser.

2

Arrange and argue

Students move images into constellations, label what they see, and add text notes where a connection needs words.

3

Keep the record

Export the board as JSON or PDF. The arrangement is the artefact: a visible record of the class’s thinking.

Research connection

The primary tool for feed-like discovery: juxtaposition and adjacency generate pattern recognition before full context is given. Part of Model A — the lesson shaped like a curated discovery — in Teaching After the Feed. Read the research →
